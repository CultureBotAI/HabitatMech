What HabitatMech adds

HabitatMech is not a re-labelling of ENVO. Most of what it holds, ENVO has no term for — and a concept that merely hangs under an ENVO term counts as new, because the identifier is minted precisely when the matched term is broader than the concept.

What the grounding status means

Every record says how well its identifier fits the source concept, separately from the recorded curation workflow status. A record can be reviewed and still honestly ungrounded — that combination is a well-formed request for a new ontology term, and there are 696 of them.

Grounding status
StatusRecordsMeaning
EXACT 1054The identifier is an exact match for the source concept.
NARROW 991The identifier is narrower than the source concept.
UNGROUNDED 955No defensible ontology term; a minted identifier and a term-request candidate.
NOT_APPLICABLE 105Not a habitat at all — a disease, quality, process or procedure.
CLOSE 92Close, but not exact and not cleanly broader or narrower.
BROAD 12The identifier is broader — the best available term, losing specificity.

This corpus is machine-generated. 1915 of 3209 records have the SEEDED workflow status. The harmonization rules are documented and tested, but a lexical match between a source label and an ontology label is a plausible grounding, not a verified one.

REVIEWED records carry a recorded workflow decision, including agent-assisted curation. These counts do not establish human review. Consult the attributed curation events on each record for its provenance.
