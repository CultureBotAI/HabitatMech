What the grounding status means

Every record says how well its identifier fits the source concept, separately from whether a human has checked it. A record can be reviewed and still honestly ungrounded — that combination is a well-formed request for a new ontology term, and there are 69 of them.

StatusRecordsMeaning

BROAD 8The identifier is broader — the best available term, losing specificity.

This corpus is machine-generated. 2751 of 3234 records have had no human review. The harmonization rules are documented and tested, but a lexical match between a source label and an ontology label is a plausible grounding, not a verified one.
