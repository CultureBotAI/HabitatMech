What HabitatMech adds

HabitatMech is not a re-labelling of ENVO. Most of what it holds, ENVO has no term for — and a concept that merely hangs under an ENVO term counts as new, because the identifier is minted precisely when the matched term is broader than the concept.

What the grounding status means

Every record says how well its identifier fits the source concept, separately from the recorded curation workflow status. A record can be reviewed and still honestly ungrounded — that combination is a well-formed request for a new ontology term, and there are 126 of them.

This corpus is machine-generated. 2516 of 3206 records have the SEEDED workflow status. The harmonization rules are documented and tested, but a lexical match between a source label and an ontology label is a plausible grounding, not a verified one.

REVIEWED records carry a recorded workflow decision, including agent-assisted curation. These counts do not establish human review. Consult the attributed curation events on each record for its provenance.
