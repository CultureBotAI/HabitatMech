sulfur-rich environment

habitatmech:BACDIVE.f655f51b59 ·OTHER ·UNGROUNDED REVIEWED

An environmental system determined by a material that is enriched in elemental sulfur, sulfide, or sulfate compounds, such as solfataras, sulfidic springs, or acid sulfate environments supporting sulfur-oxidizing and sulfate-reducing microorganisms. — HabitatMech
