Anaerobic-aerobic EBPR volatile fatty acid uptake and dual polyphosphate-PHA biopolymer cycling mechanism

Anaerobic-aerobic enhanced biological phosphorus removal (EBPR) bioreactors cycle biomass between an anaerobic zone receiving volatile fatty acids (VFAs) and an aerobic zone, selecting for polyphosphate-accumulating organisms (PAOs including Candidatus Accumulibacter, Tetrasphaera, and Candidatus Phosphoribacter) that encode polyphosphate kinase (ppk1) and hydrolyze intracellular and extracellular polyphosphate anaerobically to take up VFAs as polyhydroxyalkanoates (PHAs) before oxidizing PHAs aerobically to drive net luxury phosphorus removal.
