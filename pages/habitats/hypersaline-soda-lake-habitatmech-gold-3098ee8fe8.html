Hypersaline soda lake carbonate-alkaline brine selection and Thioalkalivibrio-Halorhodospira fcc/sox/soe/hdr/dsr sulfur oxidation and cbbL/cbbM RuBisCO carbon fixation mechanism

Hypersaline soda lakes (such as Kulunda Steppe, Wadi Natrun, and Kenyan soda lakes) maintain high concentrations of sodium carbonate/bicarbonate (stable pH > 9, optimum pH 10.0) and moderate to extremely high salinity alongside reduced sulfur compounds (sulfide, thiosulfate, elemental sulfur). These extreme haloalkaline conditions select for obligately chemolithoautotrophic and phototrophic haloalkaliphilic Ectothiorhodospiraceae (Thioalkalivibrio spp. including T. sulfidiphilus and Halorhodospira). These guilds encode flavocytochrome c (fcc), the truncated Sox system (sox), sulfite:quinone oxidoreductase (soe), heterodisulfide reductase (hdr), reverse dissimilatory sulfite reductase (dsr), and Calvin-Benson cycle form I and form II RuBisCO (cbbL, cbbM), catalyzing haloalkaline dissimilatory sulfur oxidation coupled to autotrophic CO2 assimilation.
