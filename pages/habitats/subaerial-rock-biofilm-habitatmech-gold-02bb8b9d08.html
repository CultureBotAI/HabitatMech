subaerial rock biofilm

habitatmech:GOLD.02bb8b9d08 ·TERRESTRIAL ·UNGROUNDED REVIEWED

A biofilm that develops on an atmosphere-exposed rock or stone surface at the rock-air interface and is inhabited by stress-tolerant phototrophic and heterotrophic microorganisms. — HabitatMech
