Causal graphs

Fermented fish salted amino-acid-rich matrix, Levilactobacillus/Lactiplantibacillus/Pediococcus and Tetragenococcus halophilus guild selection, and gadABC/plantaricin LPL-1/low-decarboxylase GABA synthesis and biogenic amine suppression mechanism

Traditional fermented fish products (such as Thai som-fak fermented fish cake and East Asian high-salt fish sauces) combine fish muscle proteins and free amino acids (including glutamate) with elevated sodium chloride levels. This salted, amino-acid-rich fermentation matrix selects for lactic acid bacteria (Levilactobacillus brevis, Lactiplantibacillus plantarum, Lactiplantibacillus pentosus, Pediococcus acidilactici, and Pediococcus pentosaceus) and halophilic Tetragenococcus halophilus and Staphylococcus. Levilactobacillus brevis and Pediococcus acidilactici express glutamate decarboxylase and glutamate/GABA antiporter genes (gadA, gadB, gadB1, gadB2, gadC, gadC1, gadC2) to convert glutamate into gamma-aminobutyric acid (GABA), Lactiplantibacillus plantarum LPL-1 produces the YGNGV-motif class IIa bacteriocin plantaricin LPL-1 to permeabilize and kill foodborne spoilage and pathogenic bacteria, and low-decarboxylase Tetragenococcus halophilus A003 outcompetes high amine producers to repress cadaverine, histamine, and tyramine accumulation.
