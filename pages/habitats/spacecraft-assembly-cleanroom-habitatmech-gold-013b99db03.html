Spacecraft assembly cleanroom oligotrophic surface filtering and bioburden persistence mechanism

Ultra-clean, low-biomass spacecraft assembly cleanrooms impose severe oligotrophic and surface-decontamination pressures where most detected DNA derives from non-viable cells while a resilient viable fraction (1-10% of reads) comprising endospore-forming Bacillales (Bacillus subtilis, Virgibacillus pantothenticus, Geobacillus) and hardy non-spore-forming genera (Acinetobacter, Sphingobium) persists as planetary-protection bioburden on floors and mission-critical spacecraft hardware.
