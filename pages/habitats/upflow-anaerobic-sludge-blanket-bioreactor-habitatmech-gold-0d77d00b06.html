Causal graphs

Upflow anaerobic sludge blanket granulation, syntrophic fatty-acid/aromatic oxidation, and methanogenesis mechanism

Upflow anaerobic sludge blanket (UASB) bioreactors operate under high organic loading rates with self-immobilized anaerobic granular sludge that retains biomass and selects for syntrophic fatty-acid- and aromatic-degrading bacteria (Syntrophus, Syntrophobacter, Geobacter, Pelotomaculaceae, and Syntrophorhabdaceae) employing reverse electron transfer and Re-citrate synthase in syntrophic partnership with acetoclastic and hydrogenotrophic methanogens (Methanosarcina, Methanoregula) to convert complex organic wastewaters into biomethane.
