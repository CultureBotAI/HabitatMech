indoor dust

habitatmech:GOLD.398aeb6c37 ·ENGINEERED ·UNGROUNDED REVIEWED

A dust which settles and accumulates on interior surfaces of a built structure, and which is a heterogeneous mixture of particles shed by occupants, pets and furnishings, tracked-in soil, and deposited outdoor-derived particulate matter. — HabitatMech
