Causal graphs

Partial-nitrification anammox dissolved oxygen control, NOB suppression, and stratified autotrophic nitrogen removal mechanism

Partial-nitrification anammox (PNA) bioreactors regulate airflow and maintain low dissolved oxygen (0.02-0.10 mg/L in membrane-aerated granules or <45% DO saturation in packed beds) to suppress nitrite-oxidizing bacteria (NOB such as Nitrospira) while supporting redox-stratified granules and biofilms with ammonia-oxidizing bacteria (AOB) in outer oxic layers supplying nitrite to core anammox bacteria (Candidatus Brocadia, Candidatus Kuenenia) that express hydrazine synthase and hydrazine-oxidizing enzymes to convert ammonium and nitrite via nitric oxide and hydrazine (N2H4) into dinitrogen gas.
