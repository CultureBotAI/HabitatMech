Causal graphs

Liquid water planktonic solute foraging

Conditional model of a connected marine water column containing planktonic heterotrophic bacteria and localized organic substrates. Water activity must be compatible with the focal organisms; liquid phase alone does not imply high water activity, growth, or this planktonic setting. Particles may be suspended or sinking. Extracellular hydrolysis and diffusion can create nutrient gradients, and chemotaxis can improve access where its benefits exceed its costs. A fraction of assimilated carbon supports biomass production, which can supply grazers in a microbial loop.
