Liquid water planktonic solute foraging

Bulk liquid water supplies high water activity, suspends free-living cells and organic particles, dissolves lower-molecular-weight organic substrates, and allows diffusion to form microscale nutrient patches. Extracellular enzymes hydrolyze polymers into dissolved organic matter, and motile chemotactic bacteria exploit those gradients to convert dissolved carbon into bacterial biomass within aquatic microbial loops.
