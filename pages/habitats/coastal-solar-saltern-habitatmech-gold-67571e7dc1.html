Causal graphs

Coastal solar saltern hypersaline crystallization and Haloquadratum/Salinibacter retinal-carotenoid phototrophic proton pumping mechanism

Saturated thalassic brines and crystallizer ponds in coastal solar salterns impose extreme halophilic osmotic stress and high solar irradiance, selecting for communities where the ultrathin square haloarchaeon Haloquadratum walsbyi constitutes up to 80-90% of prokaryotes alongside the extremophilic bacterium Salinibacter ruber. These dominant halophiles constitutively express bacteriorhodopsin, S-layer/halomucin cell-surface glycoproteins, and salinixanthin-retinal xanthorhodopsin proton pumps (coupled with salinixanthin-retaining beta-barrel outer-membrane porins) to harvest solar energy and maintain osmotic and charge homeostasis.
