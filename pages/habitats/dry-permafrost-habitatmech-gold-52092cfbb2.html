dry permafrost

habitatmech:GOLD.52092cfbb2 ·TERRESTRIAL ·UNGROUNDED REVIEWED

A permafrost containing negligible ice cement and extremely low liquid water availability (characteristic of hyperarid polar cold deserts such as the McMurdo Dry Valleys), where ice-free pore spaces create cold, hyperarid conditions for cryophilic microorganisms. — HabitatMech
