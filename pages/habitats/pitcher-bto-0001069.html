Carnivorous pitcher leaf phytotelma prey digestion, diazotrophy, and host nutrient provisioning mechanism

Cup-shaped carnivorous pitcher leaves (Sarracenia, Nepenthes, and Cephalotus) form self-contained aquatic phytotelmata collecting rainwater and captured insect prey whose fluid acidity and host phenotype select for convergent bacterial and eukaryotic communities that express hydrolytic enzymes and nifH-encoded nitrogenase to degrade arthropod detritus and fix atmospheric nitrogen, releasing assimilable nitrogen, phosphorus, and micronutrients for the host plant.
