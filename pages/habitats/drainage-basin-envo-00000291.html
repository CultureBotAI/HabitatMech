drainage basin

ENVO:00000291 ·resolve ·TERRESTRIAL ·EXACT REVIEWED

An extent or area of land where surface water from rain and melting snow or ice converges to a single point at lower elevation, usually the exit of the basin, where the waters join another waterbody. — ENVO
