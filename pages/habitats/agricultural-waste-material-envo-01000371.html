agricultural waste material

ENVO:01000371 ·resolve ·ENGINEERED ·EXACT REVIEWED

Agricultural waste is waste produced as a result of various agricultural operations. It includes manure and other wastes from farms, poultry houses and slaughterhouses; harvest waste; fertilizer run- off from fields; pesticides that enter into water, air or soils; and salt and silt drained from fields. — ENVO
