Causal graphs

Marine sediment sulfate sulfur cycling

A conditional sulfur-cycling model for organic-bearing marine sediment with sulfate supply and anoxic porewater, especially fine-grained coastal and continental-shelf sediment. It is not universal across marine sediment; slowly accumulating deep-ocean sediment can remain oxic to basement. Interacting sulfate-reducing populations oxidize organic carbon toward inorganic carbon while reducing sulfate to sulfide. Sulfide may be reoxidized or retained in iron sulfides and other sinks; this graph is not a complete sulfur budget or a strictly separated redox sequence.
