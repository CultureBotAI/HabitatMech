Marine sediment sulfate sulfur cycling

Marine sediment receives settling organic particles and seawater sulfate; oxygen depletion in porewater selects sulfate-reducing microorganisms that mineralize organic carbon to sulfide, and the sulfide is either reoxidized by sulfur-cycling microorganisms or trapped with reactive iron as reduced sulfur minerals.
