Causal graphs

Marine water body biological carbon pump

Marine water bodies partition microbial activity between a sunlit euphotic layer where phytoplankton and cyanobacteria fix carbon and a darker interior where sinking particles are enzymatically hydrolyzed and remineralized. Particle-associated and free-living heterotrophs convert that organic matter to dissolved inorganic carbon and regenerated nutrients while consuming oxygen along the particle flux path.
