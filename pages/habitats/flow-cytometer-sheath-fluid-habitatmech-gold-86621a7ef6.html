flow cytometer sheath fluid

habitatmech:GOLD.86621a7ef6 ·ENGINEERED ·UNGROUNDED REVIEWED

A chemical product consisting of buffered saline or ultrapure water used in a flow cytometer or fluorescence-activated cell sorter (FACS) to hydrodynamically focus suspended cells, which can introduce background reagent microbial DNA during single-cell genomics. — HabitatMech
