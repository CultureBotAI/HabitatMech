Causal graphs

Phyllosphere fluctuating methanol/succinate and oxidative-desiccation stress regime, Methylobacterium/Methylorubrum extorquens guild selection, and PhyR/Prs/CstR epiphytic colonization and stress-metabolic balancing mechanism

Aerial plant surfaces (the phyllosphere) expose epiphytic microorganisms to low and fluctuating concentrations of plant-emitted one-carbon (methanol) and multi-carbon (succinate) substrates alongside diurnal heat, desiccation, oxidative, and pH stresses. These conditions select for pink-pigmented facultative methylotrophs of the genera Methylobacterium and Methylorubrum (such as M. extorquens AM1 and PA1). During epiphytic growth on leaves, M. extorquens upregulates the two-domain sigma-factor-like response regulator PhyR and its downstream oxidative stress regulon (KatE, SodA, AhpC, Ohr, Trx, Dps), modulates phosphoribosylpyrophosphate synthetase (Prs) as a metabolic valve to channel scarce C1 carbon through the phosphoketolase pathway into biomass, and employs the orphan response receiver CstR (alongside EfgA and TtmR) to coordinate NAD+/NADH balance, formaldehyde tolerance, and stress resilience across succinate-to-methanol transitions.
