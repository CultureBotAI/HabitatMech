trough

ENVO:00000499 ·resolve ·TERRESTRIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A linear structural depression that extends laterally over a distance, while being less steep than a trench. A trough can be a narrow basin or a geologic rift. There are various oceanic troughs, troughs found under oceans; examples include the rift along the mid-oceanic ridge and the Cayman Trough. — ENVO
