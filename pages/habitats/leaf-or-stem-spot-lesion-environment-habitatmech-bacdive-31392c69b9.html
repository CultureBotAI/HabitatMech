leaf-or-stem spot lesion environment

habitatmech:BACDIVE.31392c69b9 ·OTHER ·UNGROUNDED REVIEWED

An environment associated with a plant part or small plant which is determined by a discrete necrotic or chlorotic spot lesion on a leaf or stem of a living plant, and which is formed by a localized infection that does not spread systemically through the host. — HabitatMech
