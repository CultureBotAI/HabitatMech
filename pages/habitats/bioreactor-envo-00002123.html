Bioreactor feed dilution biomass selection

Engineered bioreactor operation turns a containment vessel, liquid feed, hydraulic exchange, biomass retention, and physicochemical setpoints into a wet, nutrient-rich microbial habitat. The feed stream sets organic loading, outflow creates dilution pressure on suspended cells, retention surfaces keep attached biomass inside the vessel, and choices such as aeration, temperature, ammonia, organic loading rate, and hydraulic retention time select the community processes that transform influent into engineered products.
