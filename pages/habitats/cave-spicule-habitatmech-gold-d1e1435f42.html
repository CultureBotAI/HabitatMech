cave spicule

habitatmech:GOLD.d1e1435f42 ·TERRESTRIAL ·UNGROUNDED REVIEWED

A speleothem which consists of a macroscopic acicular mineral crystal, most commonly aragonite, precipitated subaerially from an evaporating capillary or seepage film on a cave surface or coralloid nodule. — HabitatMech
