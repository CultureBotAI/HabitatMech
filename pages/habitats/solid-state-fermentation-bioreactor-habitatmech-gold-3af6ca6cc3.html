Causal graphs

Solid-state fermentation moist solid bed heat/mass transfer and filamentous fungal enzyme production mechanism

Solid-state fermentation (SSF) bioreactors cultivate filamentous fungi (such as Aspergillus niger and Aspergillus flavus) on non-submerged, moist solid agro-industrial substrates (such as corn and palm kernel cake) where forced aeration and intermittent mixing regulate gas-solid heat and mass transfer and water activity, driving extracellular hydrolytic enzyme production and beta-mannan degradation into mannose.
