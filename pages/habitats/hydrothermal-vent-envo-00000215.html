Hydrothermal vent redox chemolithoautotrophy

Hydrothermal vents discharge geothermally heated, chemically reduced fluids into oxidized seawater, creating steep thermal and redox gradients where sulfur- and hydrogen-oxidizing chemolithoautotrophs fix inorganic carbon and form the base of vent microbial production.
