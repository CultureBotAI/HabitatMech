Diffuse-flow hydrothermal vent fluid Campylobacterota SulRS-Sox sulfur oxidation and mineral colonization mechanism

Low-temperature diffuse-flow deep-sea hydrothermal vent fluids and adjacent polymetallic sulfide minerals supply dissolved H2S, thiosulfate, and mineral-bound sulfide across subseafloor mixing gradients, selecting for chemolithoautotrophic Campylobacterota (Epsilonproteobacteria) dominated by Sulfurimonas, Sulfurovum, and Arcobacter. In Sulfurimonas, the two-component sensor kinase SulS detects thiosulfate and activates the response regulator SulR to drive transcription of the soxABXY1Z1 gene cluster alongside soxCDY2Z2 (soxB), catalyzing sulfur and thiosulfate oxidation coupled to primary production and sulfide mineral dissolution.
