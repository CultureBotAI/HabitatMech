Flooded paddy field soil root-exudate methanogenesis and rhizospheric methane oxidation mechanism

Flooded rice paddy soil compartments (bulk soil, rhizosphere, and rhizoplane) receive rice root exudates, plant litter, and root-excreted oxygen, where organic substrates and rhizosphere H2 cycling fuel mcrA-encoding hydrogenotrophic and acetoclastic methanogenic archaea while rice root radial oxygen loss induces pmoA-mediated rhizospheric methane oxidation that consumes 36-90% of biogenic CH4 within an integrated methanogen-methanotroph consortium.
