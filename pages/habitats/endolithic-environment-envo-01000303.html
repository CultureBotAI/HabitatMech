Endolithic mineral pore space UV attenuation and desiccation-buffered colonization mechanism

Translucent porous rocks and evaporite minerals (sandstone, gypsum, quartz, and carbonate) create subsurface endolithic and chasmoendolithic pore-space microhabitats in the outer centimeters of rock that attenuate excessive solar insolation and ultraviolet radiation while transmitting photosynthetically active radiation and capturing episodic snowmelt or pore moisture, selecting for specialized photoautotrophic cyanobacteria, eukaryotic algae, and co-occurring heterotrophic bacteria.
