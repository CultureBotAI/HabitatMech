  1. GROUND 2026-08-16 · claude-opus-5

    Grounded to ENVO:02000019 'bodily fluid material' (CLOSE). Term-request re-check (#12), after ENVO:01001000 showed these claims were never machine-rechecked: 'Fluids' was left ungrounded after ENVO's NCIT:C13452 'Other Body Fluids and Secretions' was refused as a residual bucket. That refusal was right and the conclusion was not: ENVO:02000019 'bodily fluid material' is a proper class — 'an organic material primarily composed of some natural bodily fluid or secretion such as blood, semen, saliva' — and that is what BacDive's bin holds, as its Digestive-fluid and Rumen-fluid children show. CLOSE rather than EXACT because BacDive's is a sampling bin and ENVO's is the material, which is the same framing difference CLOSE records elsewhere in the corpus. (source concept habitatmech:BACDIVE.6add57f329)
