Causal graphs

Floral nectar osmotic-climatic filtering, Acinetobacter-Rosenbergiella assembly, and pollinator cue modulation mechanism

Floral nectar secreted by plant nectaries presents a carbohydrate-rich, osmotically and chaotropically selective microenvironment shaped by local temperature, elevation, rainfall, and vegetation density, selecting for specialized nectar-inhabiting bacteria (dominated by Proteobacteria genera Acinetobacter and Rosenbergiella alongside Firmicutes and Actinobacteria) and yeasts (Metschnikowia spp.) that modify nectar chemistry, volatiles, nutritional content, and pollinator attractiveness.
