crustacean cuticular seta

habitatmech:GOLD.814eba298e ·HOST_ASSOCIATED ·UNGROUNDED REVIEWED

An environment associated with an animal part or small animal consisting of a cuticular bristle or hair-like projection (seta) on the exoskeleton of a crustacean (such as hydrothermal vent shrimp Rimicaris or Yeti crabs Kiwa), which serves as an attachment surface for dense filamentous epibiotic bacterial mats. — HabitatMech
