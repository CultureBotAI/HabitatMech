inland saline or alkaline aquatic environment

habitatmech:GOLD.ce244e62cd ·AQUATIC ·UNGROUNDED REVIEWED

An aquatic environment which is determined by an inland water body whose water is substantially more saline than fresh water, substantially more alkaline than circumneutral water, or both. — HabitatMech
