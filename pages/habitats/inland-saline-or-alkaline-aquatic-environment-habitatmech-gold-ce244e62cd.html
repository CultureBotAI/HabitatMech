  3. DEFINED 2026-08-25 · codex-gpt-5

    Defined as 'inland saline or alkaline aquatic environment' under ENVO:01000317 'aquatic environment'. Definition curated from the committed deep-research report for this record (#110, #185). The “or both” wording preserves GOLD's disjunctive hierarchy instead of claiming every child is simultaneously saline and alkaline. “Inland” is preferred to “non-marine” because marine-derived inland brines can remain geographically inland, while “athalassohaline” is not an exact synonym because it classifies water by ionic composition rather than location. Parent replacement is intentional: GOLD's inherited aquatic-biome edge is false for the node's operated-setting descendants, whereas ENVO's aquatic-environment genus covers both natural and engineered water-determined settings. Dropped the exact synonym 'Non-marine-Saline-and-Alkaline' (#213): it is BacDive's label for the concept GOLD spells 'Non-marine Saline and Alkaline', and the two differ only in punctuation. Both source labels survive on the record's source_attestations, where they identify which source said what; as a pair of EXACT synonyms they would read to an ENVO editor as the same string written twice.
