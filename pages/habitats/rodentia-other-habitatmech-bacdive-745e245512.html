    Confirmed UNGROUNDED: no ontology term fits this concept. Nearest broader term ENVO:01001002 'animal-associated environment' attached as a parent. Residual-host review (#192): BacDive's live vocabulary places `Rodentia (Other)` under `#Host` as the sibling of `#Muridae (Mouse/Rat)`. It is a real animal-host environment, not NCIT:C17649 `Other`; that contentless lexical target remains rejected. ENVO:01001002 is genuinely broader and the category is HOST_ASSOCIATED. The source bucket means rodent hosts not assigned to the Muridae sibling, so it stays minted and separate: merging would erase the family-rank boundary, while requesting an ontology class defined by a mutable “everything else” exclusion would make its meaning change whenever BacDive adds a sibling. Hamsters and voles remain in scope despite vernacular mouse names; the attesting Actinobacillus capsulatus includes a reported rabbit-derived type strain outside Rodentia, so source assignment is preserved as evidence rather than treated as a taxonomic guarantee.
