Causal graphs

Leaf surface phyllosphere rare-earth-element sensing and Methylobacterium XoxF-dependent methylotrophic colonization mechanism

Plant leaf surfaces (phyllosphere) release stomatal methanol and accumulate rare-earth elements (REEs such as La3+, up to 10 ug/g dry weight), selecting for pink-pigmented facultative methylotrophs such as Methylobacterium extorquens (strains PA1 and AM1). During phyllosphere colonization, M. extorquens senses REEs to induce the lanthanide-dependent PQQ methanol dehydrogenase XoxF (while repressing Ca2+-dependent mxaFI) alongside a REE-binding protein and TonB-dependent outer-membrane transporter essential for chelator-assisted REE uptake, driving rapid methanol and formaldehyde oxidation and competitive leaf colonization.
