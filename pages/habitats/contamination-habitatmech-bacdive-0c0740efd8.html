    Confirmed UNGROUNDED: no ontology term fits this concept. ENVO:00002204 'anthropogenic contamination feature' kept as an xref. Risky-grounding review (#12): the seeder made ENVO:00002204 'anthropogenic contamination feature' the identity of BacDive's 'Contamination' category — the highest-volume risky grounding at 474 strains. The term asserts two things BacDive does not: that the contamination is anthropogenic, and that the thing is a geographic feature with elevated contaminants in its materials. BacDive's category says only that a strain was isolated as a contaminant, which is as often of a product, a culture or a cleanroom as of a place. These are different concepts rather than a broader and a narrower one, so the term is not a parent either — parent_habitats asserts the parent is BROADER. Kept as an xref (#99), which records that upstream saw a connection without this repo asserting one. (source concept habitatmech:BACDIVE.0c0740efd8)
