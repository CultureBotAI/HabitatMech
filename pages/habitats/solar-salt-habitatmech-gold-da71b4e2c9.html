solar salt

habitatmech:GOLD.da71b4e2c9 ·FOOD ·UNGROUNDED REVIEWED

A sodium chloride salt crystallized by solar and wind evaporation of seawater or natural hypersaline brines in saltern crystallizer ponds and harvested for food use, often retaining viable halophilic archaea and bacteria. — HabitatMech
