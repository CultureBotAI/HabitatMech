Causal graphs

Fecal complex carbohydrate degradation, cross-feeding fermentation, and H2-dependent methanogenesis mechanism

Distal intestinal and excreted fecal matter contains undigested dietary plant cell walls, resistant starch, and host mucin glycans under strictly anoxic conditions, supporting primary polysaccharide degraders (Ruminococcus bromii on resistant starch, Ruminococcus champanellensis on cellulose, and CAZyme-rich Bacteroidota) whose fermentation releases soluble sugars, short-chain fatty acids (acetate, succinate, butyrate), and H2 that fuel syntrophic cross-feeders and H2-dependent methylotrophic/hydrogenotrophic fecal methanogens (Methanomassiliicoccus luminyensis, Methanosphaera stadtmanae).
