Anaerobic-aerobic sequencing batch EBPR cyclic substrate feeding, granule selection, and PAO phosphorus removal mechanism

Anaerobic-aerobic sequencing batch enhanced biological phosphorus removal (SBR-EBPR) bioreactors impose temporal anaerobic-aerobic feast-famine cycles in a single vessel fed with volatile fatty acids (acetate) or alternating amino acids (glutamate), driving spontaneous granule formation and selecting for morphotype I PAO aggregates dominated by Candidatus Accumulibacter clades (IC, IIB, IIC, and nitrite-tolerant subtypes) alongside coexisting Candidatus Competibacter GAOs while out-selecting nitrite-oxidizing bacteria to achieve >99% phosphorus removal.
