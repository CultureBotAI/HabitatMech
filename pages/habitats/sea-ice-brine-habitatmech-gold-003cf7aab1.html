Causal graphs

Sea-ice brine subzero hypersaline pocket selection, Colwellia/Polaribacter/Glaciecola and phage community enrichment, and CpsLip/pfa/cti/FAD membrane and lipid cryoadaptation mechanism

Seawater-derived sea-ice brines form liquid subzero hypersaline pockets within frozen sea ice where concentrated dissolved salts prevent freezing amid seasonal temperature and salinity fluctuations. These subzero hypersaline conditions select for psychrophilic bacterial assemblages (~10^5 cells mL-1) dominated by Polaribacter, Paraglaciecola, Colwellia (including Colwellia psychrerythraea 34H), Glaciecola, and Marinobacter alongside habitat-specific bacteriophages (1.3 x 10^5 to 2.1 x 10^5 mL-1). Colwellia psychrerythraea 34H and co-occurring brine bacteria and phages express cold-active lipase CpsLip (UniProtKB:Q486T5), polyunsaturated and trans-unsaturated fatty acid synthesis/isomerization genes (pfa and cti), cryoprotectant extracellular polysaccharide substances (EPS), and virally encoded fatty acid desaturase (FAD) auxiliary metabolic genes to maintain membrane fluidity and lipid hydrolysis under subzero hypersaline stress.
