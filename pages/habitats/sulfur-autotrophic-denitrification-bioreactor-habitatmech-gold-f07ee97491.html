Sulfur autotrophic denitrification elemental sulfur oxidation, alkalinity buffering, and nar/nir/nor nitrate reduction mechanism

Sulfur autotrophic denitrification (SAD) bioreactors supply elemental sulfur (S0) and alkalinity buffering (such as NaHCO3 or calcium carbide residue composites that neutralize acid production and prevent pH drops to 5.8) under low dissolved oxygen (~0.3 mg/L), enriching chemolithoautotrophic sulfur-oxidizing denitrifiers (Thiobacillus, Sulfurimonas, Sulfuritalea, and Annwoodia) that upregulate respiratory nitrate/nitrite/nitric-oxide reductases (narGHI, nir, nor), form II RubisCO (cbbM), and sulfur-oxidation enzymes (sulfide:quinone oxidoreductase and dsrC) to achieve >90-97% nitrate removal.
