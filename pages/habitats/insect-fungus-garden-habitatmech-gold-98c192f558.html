insect fungus garden

habitatmech:GOLD.98c192f558 ·TERRESTRIAL ·UNGROUNDED REVIEWED

An environmental system determined by an organism that consists of a substrate structure or gallery cultivated by fungicultural insects, such as leaf-cutter ants, fungus-growing termites, or ambrosia beetles, to farm symbiotic fungi and associated microbial consortia. — HabitatMech
