Causal graphs

Algae raceway pond paddlewheel vertical mixing, photosynthetic CO2/O2 dynamics, and phycosphere bacterial selection mechanism

Open or greenhouse-enclosed algae raceway ponds use paddlewheel circulation and baffle-induced vertical vortexes to cycle microalgae (such as Nannochloropsis and Chlamydopodium fusiforme) between bottom dark zones and top illuminated zones (flashing-light effect) while nitrate supply and open-pond exposure shape associated Bacteroidetes (Saprospiraceae, NS11-12_marine group) and Alphaproteobacteria communities that correlate with microalgal growth and photosynthetic oxygen and biomass production.
