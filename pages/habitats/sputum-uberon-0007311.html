Causal graphs

Sputum chronic airway mucus and iron-limited inflammatory milieu, mucoid and MDR Pseudomonas aeruginosa selection, and mucA/algU/algD/lasR/lasB/toxA alginate-protease-pyoverdine viscoelastic biofilm mechanism

Expectorated and lower-respiratory-tract sputum in patients with cystic fibrosis (CF) and non-CF bronchiectasis constitutes a nutrient-rich yet iron-restricted, neutrophil-infiltrated, antibiotic-exposed mucus matrix. Chronic residence in this sputum niche selects for persistent and multidrug-resistant Pseudomonas aeruginosa populations, including mucoid variants carrying loss-of-function mutations in the anti-sigma factor gene mucA. Derepression of the alternative sigma factor algU (sigma22) together with quorum-sensing regulation via lasR drives co-accumulation of alginate biosynthesis transcripts (algD), elastase (lasB), LasA protease (lasA), exotoxin A (toxA), and pyoverdine (PVD) siderophore biosynthesis, generating alginate-encased biofilms that increase sputum elasticity, viscosity, and stiffness and impair mucociliary clearance.
