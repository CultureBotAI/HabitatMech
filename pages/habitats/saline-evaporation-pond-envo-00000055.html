Causal graphs

Saline evaporation pond NaCl-saturated brine selection, Haloquadratum walsbyi and Salinibacter ruber enrichment, and Squarebop I bacteriorhodopsin / salinixanthin-xanthorhodopsin proton pumping mechanism

Solar saline evaporation and saltern crystallizer ponds maintain NaCl-saturated hypersaline brines (20-30% salt, ~309 g salts L-1) under intense solar irradiance. These extreme osmotic and light regimes select for dense halophilic communities dominated by the square-shaped haloarchaeon Haloquadratum walsbyi (50-60% of the crystallizer microbial population) and the extremely halophilic bacterium Salinibacter ruber alongside beta-carotene-rich Dunaliella. Haloquadratum walsbyi and Salinibacter ruber express retinal-based light-driven proton pumps — Squarebop I bacteriorhodopsin and salinixanthin-antenna xanthorhodopsin paired with salinixanthin-retaining outer-membrane beta-barrel porins — that generate light-driven proton motive force and compete directly with aerobic respiration for bioenergetic charge balance in hypersaline crystallizer brines.
