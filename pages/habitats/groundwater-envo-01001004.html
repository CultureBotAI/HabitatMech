Saturated subsurface aquifers host ultra-small candidate-phyla bacteria and coexisting groundwater consortia that couple anaerobic fermentation to inter-organism interactions and sequential redox metabolic handoffs; genomic evidence is drawn primarily from alluvial aquifer groundwater studies (Rifle, Colorado).
