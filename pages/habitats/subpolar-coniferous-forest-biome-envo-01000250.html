Subpolar coniferous forest ectomycorrhizal Cortinarius Mn-peroxidase humus oxidation and nitrogen-regulated carbon storage mechanism

Subpolar (boreal) coniferous forest podzols sequester nitrogen in recalcitrant organic humus horizons while trees allocate photosynthate to ectomycorrhizal fungi (notably Cortinarius acutus s.l., Cortinarius glaucopus, and Cortinarius caperatus). These fungi transcribe manganese-dependent class II peroxidases (MnP) that oxidize humus macromolecules to mobilize organic nitrogen and reduce topsoil carbon accumulation by 33%, whereas elevated inorganic ammonium/nitrogen deposition represses Mn-peroxidase activity and shifts the community toward Tylospora and Russula, accelerating humus carbon accumulation.
