Causal graphs

Permafrost subzero cryoadaptation and thaw-induced anaerobic carbon decomposition and methanogenesis mechanism

Intact permafrost imposes long-term freezing temperatures (-10 degrees C to -12 degrees C) where unfrozen intracellular and brine-film water selects for psychroactive bacteria such as Psychrobacter arcticus with cold-adapted membrane composition, cold-shock proteins, and flexible proteomes, while warming-induced permafrost thaw unlocks sequestered organic carbon under anaerobic conditions, shifting communities toward fermentative Firmicutes/Bacteroidota, competing iron/sulfate reducers, and mcrA-encoding hydrogenotrophic Methanocellales and acetoclastic Methanosarcina.
