bronchoalveolar lavage fluid

BTO:0000155 ·resolve ·HOST_ASSOCIATED ·EXACT SEEDED

A technique by which cells and fluid from bronchioles and lung alveoli are removed for diagnosis of disease or evaluation of treatment. A bronchoscope is wedged into a bronchus and sterile saline is pumped in and then removed along with the fluid and cells to be analyzed. — BTO
