soil biocrust

ENVO:01000910 ·resolve ·TERRESTRIAL ·EXACT REVIEWED

A biocrust which is formed by photosynthetic microbial assemblages colonising the surface layer of a portion of arid soil. Through their successional dynamics, these communities reduce the soil surface's albedo, impart it with stability against erosion, modify its hydrology, and promote its fertility. — ENVO
