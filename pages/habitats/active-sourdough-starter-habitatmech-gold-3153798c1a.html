Causal graphs

Sourdough cereal carbohydrate/arginine selection, Fructilactobacillus sanfranciscensis levansucrase and arginine deiminase pathway expression, and Kazachstania co-fermentation mechanism

Propagated wheat and rye sourdoughs provide cereal-derived sucrose, glucose, fructose, and arginine under acidic (pH 3.5-4.5) fermentation regimes, selecting for stable consortia of obligately heterofermentative lactic acid bacteria dominated by Fructilactobacillus (Lactobacillus) sanfranciscensis alongside acid-tolerant sourdough yeasts such as Kazachstania humilis. Fructilactobacillus sanfranciscensis expresses a cell-wall-associated levansucrase (lev, 879 aa) that converts sucrose into glucose, fructose, prebiotic 1-kestose, and levan fructan exopolysaccharides (EPS), and induces the three-enzyme arginine deiminase (ADI) pathway — arginine deiminase (ADI), ornithine transcarbamoylase (OTC), and carbamate kinase (CK) — to catabolize arginine into ornithine while enhancing acid stress tolerance and bread dough rheology.
