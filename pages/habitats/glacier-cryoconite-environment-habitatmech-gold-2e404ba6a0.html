Causal graphs

Glacier cryoconite mineral sediment and radial O2 stratification, Phormidesmis priestleyi and nitrifier/denitrifier selection, and Wzy/ABC-dependent EPS granulation and coupled N cycling mechanism

Supraglacial cryoconite holes accumulate dark mineral dust and rock particles on glacier ice surfaces under extreme cold and oligotrophic meltwater conditions. Gliding filamentous cyanobacteria including Phormidesmis priestleyi (such as Greenland Ice Sheet isolate BC1401) colonize the mineral particles and express Wzy- and ABC-dependent exopolysaccharide (EPS) biosynthesis pathways and Wps-homologous biofilm gene clusters. Secreted cyanobacterial EPS binds mineral grains into millimeter-scale oval cryoconite granules with a photosynthetically oxic cyanobacterial surface layer and an anoxic inner core enriched in organic carbon and ammonium from degrading biomass, driving active core nitrification and granule-wide denitrification while lowering glacier surface albedo.
