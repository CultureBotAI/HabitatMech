Study-scoped microbial dispersal and surface succession in buildings

Separate studies support occupant-source inference in sampled homes, ventilation-associated airborne dispersal in a university building, and surface succession after experimental restroom decontamination. These contexts are not interchangeable or universal properties of buildings. Temporal succession does not identify surviving cells as its source, and airborne observations do not establish deposition onto surfaces.
