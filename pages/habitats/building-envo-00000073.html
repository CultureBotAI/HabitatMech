Building surface human-air assembly

Buildings create dry, low-resource indoor surfaces that are repeatedly inoculated by human occupants and by outdoor air entering through ventilation. Occupancy, ventilation strategy, room connectivity, surface material, and cleaning disturbances jointly filter deposited microbes and assemble spatially distinct indoor surface microbiomes.
