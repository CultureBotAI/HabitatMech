  3. DEFINED 2026-09-10 · codex-gpt-5

    Defined as 'tuberculous granuloma' under ENVO:2100000 'anatomical entity environment'. Definition authored from the record's committed deep-research report under research/habitats/, read in full; no new paid research was commissioned. The report interprets PREGO's BTO:0002173 'tubercle' source as the tuberculosis pathology sense because the PREGO synonyms include tubercle while the high-scoring taxa are dominated by Mycobacterium tuberculosis complex genomes. The BTO, UBERON, and PO tubercle terms are homonyms for schistosome bosses, normal anatomical protrusions, and plant protrusions; ENVO lacks a tuberculous granuloma or infectious lesion class, so ENVO:2100000 'anatomical entity environment' is the tightest vendored genus for a pathological host-tissue structure. The definition is intentionally restricted to Mycobacterium tuberculosis complex granulomas because the lone Mycobacterium leprae PREGO taxon is likely lexical spillover from PREGO's co-mention method rather than evidence for a leprosy lesion habitat.
