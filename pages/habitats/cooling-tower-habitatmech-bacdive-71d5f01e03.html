cooling tower

habitatmech:BACDIVE.71d5f01e03 ·OTHER ·UNGROUNDED REVIEWED

A human construction which rejects waste heat from a recirculating water loop by evaporating part of that water into an air stream drawn through the structure, and which retains the remaining water in an open basin between passes. — HabitatMech
