Causal graphs

Urban sewage pipe conveyance selection, Arcobacter-Acinetobacter dominance, and beta-lactamase reservoir mechanism

Untreated municipal sewage conveyed through urban sewer pipe infrastructure imposes strong selective and temperature-stratified conditions distinct from human fecal inputs, selecting for coherent pipe-resident bacterial assemblages dominated by Arcobacter (~11%), Acinetobacter (~10%), Aeromonas, and Trichococcus (whereas human fecal bacteria comprise only ~13%) that mediate in-pipe waste biotransformation and harbor clinically important beta-lactamase antibiotic resistance genes (blaCTX-M, blaOXA, and blaTEM).
