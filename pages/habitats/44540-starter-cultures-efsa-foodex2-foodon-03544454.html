44540 - starter cultures (efsa foodex2)

FOODON:03544454 ·resolve ·FOOD ·CLOSE SEEDED

The group includes any type of bacteria used as starter cultures, i.e. a microbial preparation based on bacteria used in food production intentionally inoculated in a raw material to initiate and carry out fermentations. For example, starter cultures are used in meat and dairy technology. The part consumed/analysed is by default the whole marketed unit or a homogeneous representative portion.[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Starter_cultures] and [https://www.google.co.uk/search?tbm=isch&q=Starter cultures] — FOODON
