  2. DEFINED 2026-08-16 · claude-opus-5

    Defined as 'human-associated environment' under ENVO:01001002 'animal-associated environment'. The habitat is the host clade, and it is the entire content of the concept: GOLD and BacDive both organise host-associated isolates by host, and every downstream anatomical path hangs off it. HabitatMech deliberately does not ground these to animal-associated environment, because every clade would then merge onto one record and the distinction the sources are making would be lost. ENVO already has this shape at plant-, animal- and fungi-associated environment; this asks for the same pattern one level down. This is the single largest ungrounded concept in the corpus.
