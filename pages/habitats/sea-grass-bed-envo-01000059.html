Causal graphs

Seagrass bed meristem radial oxygen loss, sulfate reduction, and sulfide-oxidizing rhizosphere mechanism

Marine seagrass beds (Zostera marina, Zostera japonica, Zostera muelleri, and Ruppia sinensis) release dissolved organic carbon and radial O2 loss (ROL) from root and basal leaf meristems to form oxic microzones within anoxic sulfide-rich marine sediments, enriching sulfate-reducing bacteria (Desulfocapsaceae and root-apical-meristem SRB that generate H2S while contributing to nitrogen fixation) and recruiting sulfur-oxidizing bacteria (Sulfurovaceae, Rhodobacteraceae, and Chromatiales) in oxic microniches that mitigate phytotoxic sulfide stress.
