Freshwater biome epilimnetic pH, dissolved organic matter photolysis, and bacterioplankton niche partitioning mechanism

Epilimnetic surface waters across freshwater lakes and rivers span a broad pH gradient (pH 3.8-8.5) with humic and algal-derived dissolved organic matter (DOM) whose solar photolysis generates singlet oxygen (1O2), selecting for singlet-oxygen-tolerant Betaproteobacteria (Polynucleobacter necessarius, Polynucleobacter acidiphobus/difficilis, and Limnohabitans R-BT065 lineage) and Alphaproteobacteria (Novosphingobium acidiphilum) while inhibiting singlet-oxygen-sensitive ultramicroscopic freshwater Actinobacteria (acI-B / Candidatus Nanopelagicales).
