Milk carbohydrate lactic-acid assembly

Mammary glands secrete milk as a nutrient-rich aqueous emulsion containing lactose, oligosaccharides, fat globules, proteins, antimicrobial bioactives, and low-abundance milk microbiota. Milk sugars select saccharolytic lactic-acid bacteria and bifidobacteria, while fermentation converts carbohydrates into lactate and acetate that can acidify the milk habitat and reshape downstream community structure.
