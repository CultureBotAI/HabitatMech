Brackish water salinity-transition osmoregulation

Brackish water forms where freshwater inflow and seawater intrusion create intermediate salinity and tidal salinity variability. These osmotic shifts filter freshwater and marine bacterioplankton, select osmoregulatory transport and compatible-solute strategies, and allow brackish-adapted estuarine populations to establish when growth outpaces hydrologic flushing.
