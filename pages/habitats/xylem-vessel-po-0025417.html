Causal graphs

Plant xylem vessel pit membrane pectin/xyloglucan barrier, Xylella fastidiosa colonization, and PglA polygalacturonase / EngXCA1-EngXCA2 endoglucanase / RpfF-DSF systemic movement mechanism

Plant xylem vessels transport water and mineral sap across vascular networks partitioned by intervessel pit membranes (PMs) composed of homogalacturonan pectins and fucosylated xyloglucan/cellulosic polymers. This xylem-limited vascular niche selects for specialized vascular bacteria including Xylella fastidiosa, the causal agent of Pierce's disease in Vitis vinifera grapevines. Xylella fastidiosa encodes and secretes polygalacturonase PglA (pglA) and beta-1,4-endoglucanases EngXCA1 and EngXCA2 under the regulation of the diffusible signal factor (DSF) synthase RpfF (rpfF). PglA and EngXCA1/EngXCA2 enzymatically digest intervessel pit membrane pectins and fucosylated xyloglucans, breaching pit pore membranes to enable vessel-to-vessel systemic colonization while reducing stem hydraulic conductivity and air-seeding thresholds.
