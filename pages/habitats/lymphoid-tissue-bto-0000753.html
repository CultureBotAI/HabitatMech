lymphoid tissue

BTO:0000753 ·resolve ·HOST_ASSOCIATED ·EXACT SEEDED

Tissue that is particularly rich in lymphocytes (and accessory cells such as macrophages and reticular cells), particularly the lymph nodes, spleen, thymus, Peyer's patches, pharyngeal tonsils, adenoids, and (in birds) the Bursa of Fabricius. — BTO
