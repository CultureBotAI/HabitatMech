Biofilm EPS gradient structuring

Biofilms arise when microbial cells adhere to a substratum, remain together as an aggregate, and secrete an extracellular polymeric matrix. The matrix anchors cells and public goods near a surface, retains extracellular enzymes, slows solute transport, and creates oxygen and nutrient gradients that differentiate the biofilm into physiologically distinct microzones.
