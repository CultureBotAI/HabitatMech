R2A agar medium

habitatmech:GOLD.4a4dd0001d ·ENGINEERED ·UNGROUNDED REVIEWED

A culture medium solidified with agar and formulated with low concentrations of yeast extract, proteose peptone, casamino acids, glucose, soluble starch, and pyruvate (Reasoner's 2A agar) for recovering heterotrophic bacteria from potable and environmental waters. — HabitatMech
