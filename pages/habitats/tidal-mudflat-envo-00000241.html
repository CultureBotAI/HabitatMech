Causal graphs

Tidal mudflat periodic tidal redox alternation, Candidatus Electrothrix cable bacteria electrogenic sulfur oxidation, and Desulfobulbaceae/SRB HS--SO42- redox connector mechanism

Intertidal mudflat sediments experience periodic alternation of tidal flooding and air exposure alongside salinity fluctuations and vertical oxygen-sulfide gradients. These tidal redox and sulfide replenishment regimes select for centimeter-long multicellular filamentous cable bacteria (Candidatus Electrothrix and Ca. Electronema in the family Desulfobulbaceae) interacting with sulfate-reducing bacteria (such as Desulfobacca spp. and Desulfatiglans spp.). Cable bacteria express periplasmic cytochromes, conductive periplasmic fibers, reversed canonical sulfate-reduction enzymes, and Wood-Ljungdahl CO2 fixation and N2 fixation pathways, catalyzing electrogenic sulfur oxidation (e-SOx) coupled to cathodic oxygen respiration and maintaining a dynamic HS--SO42- redox connector that drives deep sediment oxidation and organic pollutant biodegradation.
