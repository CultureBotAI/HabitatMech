insect honeydew

habitatmech:GOLD.e799433ca6 ·HOST_ASSOCIATED ·UNGROUNDED REVIEWED

An environmental material consisting of sugar-rich liquid excreted from the hindgut of phloem-sap-feeding hemipteran insects (such as aphids, whiteflies, and scale insects) that contains insect gut symbionts and supports phyllosphere microbes. — HabitatMech
