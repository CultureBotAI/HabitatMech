deep marine sediment

ENVO:00002113 ·resolve ·AQUATIC ·EXACT SEEDED

Sediment that accumulates in the flat or very gently sloping areas of the deep ocean basin floor. The three main types of deep marine sediment, also known as pelagic sediment, are siliceous oozes, calcareous oozes, and red clays. — ENVO
