leaf nodule

habitatmech:GOLD.3988adca86 ·HOST_ASSOCIATED ·UNGROUNDED REVIEWED

An environment associated with a plant part or small plant consisting of a specialized symbiotic nodule or gland structure within the lamina of a plant leaf (as in Psychotria, Pavetta, Ardisia, or Dioscorea) that houses obligate bacterial leaf endophytes. — HabitatMech
