Causal graphs

Petroleum reservoir anaerobic hydrocarbon biodegradation and methanogenic heavy-oil conversion mechanism

Subsurface petroleum reservoirs and entrapped microliter formation-water droplets provide crude-oil n-alkanes and aromatic hydrocarbons under anoxic conditions below ~80 degrees C, selecting for anaerobic hydrocarbon-degrading bacteria and hydrogenotrophic methanogenic archaea at oil-water interfaces that sequentially remove n-alkanes, generate stoichiometric biogenic methane, and convert light crude oil into heavy oil.
