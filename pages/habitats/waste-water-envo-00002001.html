Wastewater organic-nitrogen biotreatment

Anthropogenic wastewater supplies biodegradable organic matter and ammonium that select for engineered microbial consortia in which oxic heterotrophic oxidation, ammonia oxidation, nitrite oxidation, and anoxic denitrification jointly remove carbon and nitrogen.
