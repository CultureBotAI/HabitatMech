serpentinization-hosted carbonate hydrothermal vent chimney

habitatmech:GOLD.1208484062 ·AQUATIC ·UNGROUNDED REVIEWED

A marine hydrothermal vent chimney which is built of brucite and calcium and magnesium carbonates precipitated where low-temperature, hyperalkaline, hydrogen- and methane-rich fluid produced by serpentinization of ultramafic rock mixes with seawater. — HabitatMech
