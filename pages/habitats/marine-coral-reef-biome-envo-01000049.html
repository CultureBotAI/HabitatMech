marine coral reef biome

ENVO:01000049 ·resolve ·AQUATIC ·EXACT SEEDED

The marine coral reef biome comprises constructional wave-resistant entities which are primarily built by corals and are often cemented together. The growth of these structures is aided by zooxanthellae, algae that are symbiotic with the reef-building corals. — ENVO
