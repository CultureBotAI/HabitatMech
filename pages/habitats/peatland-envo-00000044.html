Causal graphs

Acidic Sphagnum peatland hydrogenotrophic methanogenesis and type II alphaproteobacterial methane oxidation mechanism

Rain-fed (ombrotrophic) Sphagnum-dominated peatlands maintain acidic (pH 4.0-4.5), low-sodium, potassium-poor anoxic conditions that select for salt-sensitive acidophilic hydrogenotrophic methanogens such as Methanoregula boonei 6A8 (utilizing H+ gradients for ATP synthesis and redundant trk/kdp/kup potassium uptake systems to reduce H2/CO2 to CH4), while biogenic methane flux supports dense acidophilic type II Alphaproteobacteria methanotrophs (Methylocystis, Methylocapsa acidiphila, and Methylocella palustris) expressing particulate methane monooxygenase (pmoA).
