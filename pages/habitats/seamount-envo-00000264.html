seamount

ENVO:00000264 ·resolve ·TERRESTRIAL ·EXACT SEEDED

A mountain rising from the ocean seafloor that does not reach to the water's surface (sea level), and thus is not an island. Typically formed from volcanoes that rise abruptly and are usually found rising from a seafloor of 1,000 - 4,000 meters depth. Independent features that rise to at least 1,000 meters above the seafloor. — ENVO
