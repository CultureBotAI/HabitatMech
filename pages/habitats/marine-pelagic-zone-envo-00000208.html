Marine pelagic zone oligotrophic nutrient limitation, Prochlorococcus-SAR11 metabolic coupling, and phosphonate synthesis mechanism

The sunlit oligotrophic marine pelagic zone imposes strong nitrogen and phosphorus depletion alongside high solar irradiance and salinity gradients, selecting for streamlined populations of Prochlorococcus and SAR11 (Pelagibacterales) that account for over half of euphotic-zone genome equivalents, where Prochlorococcus supplies organic carbon and glycine betaine to support SAR11 growth and C1 metabolism while roughly 15% of surface bacterioplankton (primarily Prochlorococcus and SAR11) encode phosphonate biosynthesis enzymes to form cell-surface phosphonoglycoproteins that mitigate grazing and viral lysis.
