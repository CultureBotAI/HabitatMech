hemipteran midgut filter chamber

habitatmech:GOLD.6e59a6cbb1 ·HOST_ASSOCIATED ·UNGROUNDED REVIEWED

A midgut anatomical specialization in sap-feeding hemipteran insects in which the anterior midgut, posterior midgut, and proximal Malpighian tubules are enclosed within a common sheath to bypass excess dietary water directly to the hindgut. — HabitatMech
