Fungi-associated hyphae mycosphere dispersal

Fungal structures create host-associated microsites by extending hyphae through soil and organic matter, releasing fungal exudates, exposing chitin-rich cell walls, and forming hydrated surfaces that bacteria can colonize. These hyphal traits recruit mycosphere bacteria, provide fungal highways for motile dispersers, support fungus-feeding chitinolytic bacteria, and contain intracellular niches for endofungal symbionts.
