Causal graphs

Rhizosphere root exudate recruitment and disease-suppressive microbiome mechanism

Root-adjacent soil receives photosynthetically fixed carbon and exported metabolites via root exudates, driving chemical signaling and recruitment of plant-beneficial bacteria (Proteobacteria, Firmicutes, and Actinobacteria) that suppress soil-borne fungal root pathogens via γ-proteobacterial nonribosomal peptide synthetase antibiotic biosynthesis.
