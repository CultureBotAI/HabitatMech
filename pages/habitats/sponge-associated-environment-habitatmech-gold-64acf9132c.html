  1. CONFIRM_UNGROUNDED 2026-08-16 · claude-opus-5

    Confirmed UNGROUNDED: no ontology term fits this concept. NCBITaxon:6040 'Porifera' kept as an xref. Host-taxon reversal (#114): this was NOT_APPLICABLE on the reasoning that 'a host taxon is not a habitat'. That reasoning is wrong, and the corpus already contradicted it — the same kind of concept was treated as a habitat for Sponge, Nematoda, Reptilia, Mammals, Birds and Fish, which keep their own identity and a term request for an <X>-associated environment. Host-associated > Porifera was NOT_APPLICABLE while its own child Porifera > Sponge was a term request.An organism acting as a host IS a habitat: it is where the microbe lives, and ENVO models exactly this at plant-associated, animal-associated and fungi-associated environment. What is not a habitat is the TAXON TERM — a phylum is a class of organisms, not a place — so the taxon stays an xref (#99) and the concept keeps its own identity as a term-request candidate. NOT_APPLICABLE said the concept is not a habitat, which is a stronger and false claim. (source concept habitatmech:GOLD.64acf9132c)

  5. DEFINED 2026-09-02 · claude-opus-5

    Defined as 'sponge-associated environment' under ENVO:01001002 'animal-associated environment'. Definition drafted from the record's deep-research report under research/habitats/ and read against it; the report's proposed wording, genus and near-miss table are the evidence. Parented to the aquatic-invertebrate term rather than to animal-associated environment, which the report identified as the more specific existing genus. Genus moved from ENVO:01001176 'environment associated with an aquatic invertebrate' to ENVO:01001002 (#210), with the whole aquatic-invertebrate host family rather than record by record - #210 was filed over three records and the genus is actually held by five, so fixing only the three would have rebuilt the same split it reports. Both terms are true of the concept, so this chooses between two true genera on what each drags in: ENVO asserts ENVO:01001176 under ENVO:01001002 AND ENVO:01001055 'environment associated with an animal part or small animal', so the tighter genus makes every descendant inherit a size-and-partonomy restriction that is false of a whole adult sponge, ascidian, oyster, giant clam or sea urchin. ENVO's own modelling is the decisive evidence: ENVO:01001179 'cnidarian-associated environment' - the closest analogue, a taxon-scoped class over aquatic invertebrates - is asserted under ENVO:01001002 ALONE, and ENVO:01001176 has no children in the slice at all. Not carried as an xref: it is genuinely broader, so recording it would re-import the ancestry this declines.
