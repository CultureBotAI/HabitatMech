Schema reference

HabitatMech - Microbial Habitat and Environment Schema. Generated by just render from src/habitatmech/schema/habitatmech.yaml, which states no version, and the modules it imports: . Each class lists the slots LinkML resolves for it, inherited and imported ones included. Descriptions are the schema's own wording and are shown without links: the identifiers in them are format examples. Schema licence: BSD-3-Clause.

LinkML schema for modeling microbial habitats and environments. Each HabitatRecord is a single habitat or environmental context — "hypersaline lake", "human large intestine", "activated sludge", "fermented seafood" — grounded in ENVO / UBERON / FOODON / BTO and harmonized across the three source vocabularies that describe where microbes are found:

* the JGI GOLD ecosystem classification (5-level ecosystem paths), * BacDive isolation sources (where type strains were isolated from), * PREGO habitat-organism associations (text-mined and curated).

The harmonization is the point. The same habitat is called "Environmental > Aquatic > Marine > Intertidal zone" by GOLD, "Marine-intertidal" by BacDive, and ENVO:00000316 by PREGO; a HabitatRecord carries all three as `source_attestations` on one ontology-grounded concept, with the per-source assertion counts that say how much data sits behind each.

Records are seeded from the inventories in `data/raw/` (extracted from kg-microbe by `scripts/extract_source_inventory.py`) and curated incrementally, mirroring the dismech / TraitMech / CultureMech workflow: one YAML per habitat, an append-only curation trail, validation via `just validate-all`.

The "Mech" half is `causal_graphs`: evidence-backed mechanism sketches linking a habitat's physicochemical conditions to the microbial adaptations they select for. Unlike the seeded descriptive fields, every causal edge must carry a citation.

HabitatRecord

A single microbial habitat or environmental context, harmonized across the GOLD / BacDive / PREGO source vocabularies and grounded in an environment or anatomy ontology. One YAML per record.

HabitatSourceEnum

Upstream vocabulary or dataset an attestation comes from.

GOLD
JGI GOLD ecosystem classification (5-level ecosystem paths).
BACDIVE
BacDive isolation sources for type strains.
PREGO
PREGO habitat-organism associations (ENVO and BTO terms).
MADIN
Madin et al.'s literature-curated prokaryote phenotypic trait compilation, whose `isolation_source` column kg-microbe transforms into habitat-taxon associations. Curated from publications at species level, which makes it independent of PREGO's text mining and of BacDive's strain records.
ENVIRONMENTS_TABLE
kg-microbe's environment physicochemical parameter table (data/raw/environments.csv upstream), which supplies the water/nutrient/pH/salinity/temperature/pressure bands.
ONTOLOGY
The grounding ontology itself (ENVO / UBERON / FOODON / BTO) — used when the record exists because the term exists, independent of any source vocabulary asserting it.
CURATOR
Curator-introduced, with no upstream source attestation.

AssertionUnitEnum

What a `SourceAttestation.assertion_count` counts. Recorded explicitly because the sources count different things and the numbers are not summable across sources.

ORGANISM
GOLD organism records (gold:Go*).
STUDY
GOLD study records (gold:Gs*).
BIOSAMPLE
GOLD biosample records (gold:Gb*).
STRAIN
BacDive strain records.
TAXON
Distinct taxa associated with the habitat (PREGO, MADIN). The unit is the same for both and the counts are the same kind of thing, but the granularity is not: PREGO's taxa are largely strain-level and Madin's are species-level, so a larger PREGO count does not mean a broader habitat. Kept as one unit rather than split, because inventing a second name for "distinct taxa" would imply a difference in what is being counted rather than in how finely.
ASSERTION
Individual assertions, where the asserting entity is not distinguished.

HabitatCategoryEnum

Coarse-grained habitat bucket, used for filesystem layout and reporting. The top-level split follows GOLD (Environmental / Host-associated / Engineered) because that is the vocabulary with the deepest hierarchy; environmental habitats are further split by realm, and FOOD and CLINICAL are broken out because BacDive and FOODON populate them heavily and lumping them into Engineered / Host-associated would hide them.

TERRESTRIAL
Soils, sediments, subsurface, rock, and other land environments.
AQUATIC
Marine, freshwater, and other water-column or aquatic-sediment environments.
AIR
Atmospheric and aerosol environments.
HOST_ASSOCIATED
Habitats defined by a living host — plant, animal, or microbial — including anatomical sites (UBERON/BTO) and rhizosphere/phyllosphere.
ENGINEERED
Built or managed environments: bioreactors, wastewater treatment, built environment, industrial process water, agriculture.
FOOD
Food and beverage matrices, including fermented products (FOODON).
CLINICAL
Clinical and diagnostic sample contexts (abscess, blood culture, wound) — where a strain was isolated in a medical setting, as distinct from the host anatomy itself.
OTHER
Does not fit the buckets above, or too coarse to place.

WATER_VARIABILITY
Temporal variability of water availability (e.g. "occasionally dry").
NUTRIENTS
Inorganic nutrient availability.
ORGANIC_MATTER
Organic carbon availability.
GRADIENTS
Strength of spatial physicochemical gradients.
STRUCTURAL_COMPLEXITY
Physical structure and surface availability of the matrix.
PRESSURE
Hydrostatic or lithostatic pressure.
TEMPERATURE
Temperature level.
TEMPERATURE_VARIABILITY
Temporal variability of temperature.
SALINITY
Salinity level.
SALINITY_VARIABILITY
Temporal variability of salinity.
PH
Acidity or alkalinity.
OXYGEN
Oxygen availability / redox state.
OTHER
An axis not covered above; describe it in `source` or on the record.

GroundingStatusEnum

Quality of the link between this record's `identifier` and the source concept(s) it harmonizes. Tracked separately from `mapping_status` so a reviewed-but-ungrounded record can be stated honestly rather than being forced into a bad ontology term.

EXACT
The identifier is an exact match for the source concept.
BROAD
The identifier is broader than the source concept — the best available term, but it loses specificity.
NARROW
The identifier is narrower than the source concept.
CLOSE
Close but not exact, and not cleanly broader or narrower.
UNGROUNDED
No defensible ontology term found; the record carries a minted `habitatmech:` identifier and is a candidate for a new ENVO term.
NOT_APPLICABLE
The source concept is not a habitat (e.g. a treatment or a quality that leaked into an isolation-source vocabulary), so grounding it as one would be wrong.

CausalNodeTypeEnum

Broad categories for nodes in habitat mechanism graphs. Deliberately a superset of TraitMech's enum — the shared values keep graphs comparable across the two repos, and HABITAT / ENVIRONMENTAL_PARAMETER / TAXON / COMMUNITY are the habitat-side additions.

HABITAT
A habitat or environmental context (a HabitatMech or ENVO term).
ENVIRONMENTAL_PARAMETER
A physicochemical axis of the habitat (salinity, pH, temperature).
TAXON
An organism or clade.
COMMUNITY
An assemblage of interacting microbial populations in a shared setting, potentially spanning multiple taxa. Not a single organism or clade, and not the process performed by the assemblage.
TRAIT
A microbial trait (a TraitMech or METPO term).
PATHWAY
A pathway or pathway-like mechanism.
ENVIRONMENTAL_FACTOR
An environmental exposure or stressor that is not a measured parameter.
EXPERIMENTAL_FACTOR
An assay, perturbation, or experimental condition.
GENE_OR_PROTEIN
A gene, protein, gene product, or protein complex.
CHEMICAL
A chemical entity.
CELLULAR_LOCALIZATION
A cellular component or localization.
MOLECULAR_FUNCTION
A molecular activity or function.
BIOLOGICAL_PROCESS
A biological process.
STATE
A bioenergetic or molecular state of the cell (e.g. proton motive force, turgor pressure) mediating a mechanism. Distinct from a process: the state is the steady value, not its establishment.
QUALITY
A measurable attribute of a cellular component (e.g. membrane fluidity) that modulates the mechanism.
CAPACITY
A metabolic or functional capacity — a reservoir-of-X notion that is neither a single chemical species nor a process (e.g. reducing power).
COMMUNITY_PROCESS
A process at the community rather than the cell level (e.g. syntrophy, succession, biofilm formation) — habitats select on these directly.

ProteinExampleRoleEnum

How a single UniProtKB polypeptide exemplar relates to the encompassing GENE_OR_PROTEIN mechanism node.

subunit
One polypeptide subunit or component of a multisubunit enzyme or transporter complex represented by the node.
family_member
A distinct protein family member or paralog within a broader channel, enzyme, or transporter class represented by the node.
strain_exemplar
A strain- or species-specific ortholog exemplar of the protein represented by the node.
isoprotein
One isoform or multigene-family isoprotein variant of the protein represented by the node.

DiscussionKindEnum

Kind of unresolved / in-progress item captured by a Discussion. Knowledge gaps are represented as a discussion kind so they reuse the shared pointer, evidence, and lifecycle machinery, while optional proposed experiments capture how a gap could be resolved.

OPEN_QUESTION
An unresolved scientific question posed by curators or experts.
KNOWLEDGE_GAP
A missing causal, evidentiary, model-system, or measurement assertion whose resolution would materially improve the record.
CONTROVERSY
A live disagreement or competing interpretation between published positions.
CURATION_TODO
A curation task captured inline (e.g. "ingredient needs CHEBI refinement").
EMERGING_HYPOTHESIS
A recently reported hypothesis under active discussion in the community.
INTERPRETATION
A discussion about how to interpret existing evidence or model an edge.
HUMAN_MODEL_MISMATCH
A gap where evidence exists in one system but its fidelity to the target context is uncertain (e.g. an in-vitro/model result whose transfer to the in-situ or host-associated setting is unverified).

DiscussionStatusEnum

Lifecycle status for a Discussion.

OPEN
Posed but not yet under active discussion.
UNDER_DISCUSSION
Actively being discussed in one or more linked venues.
RESOLVED
Closed with a documented resolution; kept for provenance.
ARCHIVED
No longer active and not resolved (deferred, stale, or superseded).

SupportLevelEnum

How a SupportingReference bears on the claim it is attached to (mirrors the supports semantics already used in the Mech EvidenceItem models).

SUPPORT
The source supports the claim.
REFUTE
The source contradicts the claim.
PARTIAL
The source partially supports the claim or supports it with caveats.
NO_EVIDENCE
The source is relevant context but does not directly bear on the claim.
WRONG_STATEMENT
The cited statement was found to be incorrect (kept for provenance).

DatasetTypeEnum

Type of dataset or data resource. Canonical UNION of CultureMech's and CommunityMech's enums plus microbial additions. Migration map (old → this): CultureMech values carry over unchanged; CommunityMech GENOME→GENOMICS, METAGENOME→METAGENOMICS, METATRANSCRIPTOME→METATRANSCRIPTOMICS, METAPROTEOME→METAPROTEOMICS (AMPLICON_16S / AMPLICON_ITS / METABOLOMICS / PHENOTYPE / MULTI_OMICS / OTHER are unchanged).

GENOMICS
Isolate / single-organism genome data. (CultureMech GENOMICS; CommunityMech GENOME)
METAGENOMICS
Shotgun metagenome sequencing. (CommunityMech METAGENOME)
AMPLICON_16S
16S rRNA marker-gene amplicon sequencing.
AMPLICON_ITS
ITS marker-gene amplicon sequencing.
AMPLICON_OTHER
Marker-gene amplicon sequencing other than 16S/ITS (e.g. 18S, rpoB).
TRANSCRIPTOMICS
Single-organism RNA sequencing / expression.
METATRANSCRIPTOMICS
Community-level RNA sequencing. (CommunityMech METATRANSCRIPTOME)
PROTEOMICS
Single-organism protein expression profiling.
METAPROTEOMICS
Community-level proteomics. (CommunityMech METAPROTEOME)
METABOLOMICS
Metabolite profiling.
FLUXOMICS
Metabolic flux profiling.
PHENOMICS
High-throughput phenotype profiling.
PHENOTYPE
Phenotype / trait measurement collection (e.g. growth, biochemical).
MULTI_OMICS
Integrated multi-omics profiling.
OTHER
A dataset type not covered by the above.
