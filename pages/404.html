If you followed a link to a habitat record, the most likely explanation is that curation moved it. A record's page is named after its label and its identifier, and improving either — adopting the grounding ontology's own label, or re-grounding the concept onto a better term — changes both.

Records retired that way keep a stub at their old address pointing to wherever their source concepts went, so a dead link usually still resolves. Reaching this page means the address never existed, or predates that stub being written.
