## Findings

1. **Major: mode-specific graph claims are insufficiently scoped at the generic
   bioreactor root.** Overlay lines5-11 and 68-87 make liquid, wet, nutrient-rich,
   actively managed operation appear class-defining. Later flow/dilution edges
   similarly need continuous-flow assumptions. ENVO covers a wider containment
   class, and the cited literature explicitly concerns narrower operating modes.
   The already-qualified biofilm and anaerobic branches should remain qualified;
   do not delete them merely because they are examples. Owner:
   `curation/causal_graphs/bioreactor.yaml`.
2. **Major: a microbial community is typed as a taxon.** Node
   `retained_microbiome` at overlay lines48-50 is a mixed community, not an
   organism or clade as required by `CausalNodeTypeEnum.TAXON` at schema line835.
   This mis-types the object of multiple selection edges despite schema-valid
   syntax. Owner: overlay plus a deliberate local schema/type extension if a
   community entity must remain. COMMUNITY_PROCESS is not a substitute for the
   community itself, nor is a single taxon a substitute for the original claim.
3. **Minor: methanogenic substrate wording conflates carbon and electron-donor
   roles.** Overlay lines267-277 uses a node containing hydrogen but describes
   the inputs as acetate or reduced one-carbon intermediates. H2 has no carbon;
   Hattori explicitly distinguishes hydrogen/CO2 and acetate pathways. Clarify
   the description and, if the diagram is meant to represent those routes,
   separate their inputs. Owner: the same overlay.
