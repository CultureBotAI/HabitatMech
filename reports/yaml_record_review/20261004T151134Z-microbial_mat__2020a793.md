[ENVO:01000008 microbial mat](https://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/ENVO_01000008),
whose current official OWL defines a layered microbial sheet at material
interfaces. ENVO:01000157 microbial mat material is separately derived
material, not an automatic replacement identity for the intact mat.

Retaining the path-specific mint prevents conflation with every mat from
other settings. However, the entire other parent, `transient_pool.yaml`,
is GOLD.d39a2ed099, Transient pool, UNGROUNDED/SEEDED, with no authored
associated-environment definition. A mat situated in a pool is not itself
a pool. The source context is valid, but its promotion to is-a is not.
The pool parent's own fresh-water superclass is a separate record question;
correcting it would not fix this mat-to-pool edge.

The generated NARROW value matches the resolver's internal anti-conflation
route, but not the schema's declared comparison endpoints. This is addressed
separately below; do not confuse a sound retained identity with a sound
machine-readable mapping predicate.
