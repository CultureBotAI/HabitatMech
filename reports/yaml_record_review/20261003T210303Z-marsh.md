denotes a wetland ecosystem with predominantly herbaceous communities.

`data/habitats/terrestrial/freshwater_marsh.yaml`; no duplicate should be made.

The inspected [EPA marsh description](https://www.epa.gov/wetlands/classification-and-types-wetlands)
supports the herbaceous wetland interpretation and explicitly includes fresh,

renamed or narrowed to accommodate GOLD. The source path supplies the
freshwater scope; no missing sample salinity measurements are invented.
