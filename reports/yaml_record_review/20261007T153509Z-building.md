## Findings

No blocker. Five major and two minor findings:

1. **Major: wrong sampling compartment in citation attribution.** The E1 note
   attached to `building_contains_indoor_surfaces` misdescribes an air survey.
   Owner: `curation/causal_graphs/building.yaml`.
2. **Major: graph certainty and scope exceed cited evidence.** The edge audit
   above identifies unsupported or over-scoped mechanism clauses, including
   physical-material filtering and unqualified control/reseeding predicates.
   Preserve supported bounded associations; do not present hypotheses as
   demonstrated mechanisms. Owner: the same causal overlay.
3. **Major: surface-specific environmental bands are attached to the whole
   building.** Nine source-qualified but structurally unscoped values coexist
   with broader building contexts. Owners: the governed upstream
   `data/raw/environments.csv` mapping, `src/habitatmech/extract.py` and
   `src/habitatmech/seed.py`; a schema change may be needed if contextual bands
   are retained rather than routed to an appropriate surface habitat.
4. **Major: material taxon-name drift.** `NCBITaxon:1193060` carries a different
   species name from current NCBI. `115979` additionally needs a governed
   current-name/synonym policy. Owners: upstream Madin/NCBITaxon label inputs and
   `src/habitatmech/extract.py`; regenerated `data/raw/madin_habitat_taxa.tsv`
   carries the fix. Do not change stable IDs merely to match stale names.
5. **Major: graph populations are assigned incompatible node types.** The three
   nodes identified above violate their enum meanings. Owner: the overlay;
   `src/habitatmech/schema/habitatmech.yaml` only if an explicit community/population
   representation is introduced through a separately governed schema change.
6. **Minor: snippets are weak locators and include two non-verbatim strings.**
   Replace generic/title-only snippets with short exact supporting passages and
   precise section locators, keeping interpretation in notes. Owner: the overlay.
7. **Minor: graph session audit is missing from the searched history tree.** The
   generated September ADD_CAUSAL_GRAPH event exists, but is not a full session
   history. Future curation should append an honest history record rather than
   retroactively inventing model/session details. Owner: `history/` using the
   maintained history scaffolder and schema.
