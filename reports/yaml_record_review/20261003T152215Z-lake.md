the definition admits water or other liquids, while `ENVO:01000617` lentic

flow, under `ENVO:00000063` water body. The latter parent is appropriate for
ordinary water lakes, not automatically for the definition's wider class.
[Official ENVO OWL](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/EnvironmentOntology/envo/master/envo.owl),
[lentic water body](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ols4/api/ontologies/envo/terms?obo_id=ENVO%3A01000617).

This is a real scope distinction, not a hypothetical interpretation of
other liquid: NASA's inspected Cassini account describes methane-filled
lakes on Titan. That example tests the definition's extension only; it
does not place PREGO organisms on Titan or assert extraterrestrial life.
Resolve the upstream text/hierarchy scope explicitly, rather than deleting
valid water-lake hierarchy or silently narrowing an externally owned term.
[NASA Cassini results](https://www.nasa.gov/missions/cassini/nasas-cassini-reveals-surprises-with-titans-lakes/).

Official ENVO types `catch basin`, `open water` and `tarn` as narrow synonyms,
and `broad`, `llyn`, `loch`, `lochan`, `lough`, `mere`, `mortlake` and
`pasteuer lake` as related. None of these eleven assertions is exact.
