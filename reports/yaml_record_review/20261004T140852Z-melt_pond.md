## Identity and Grounding

Current [ENVO:03000040](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ols4/api/ontologies/envo/terms?obo_id=ENVO%3A03000040)
is active and agrees with the record's label and definition. It denotes a
water body formed by localized melting in a cavity on glacier or sea ice.
Its actual ontology superclass is
[ENVO:00000063 water body](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ols4/api/ontologies/envo/terms?obo_id=ENVO%3A00000063),
not a pond-only or solid-ice class. AQUATIC is supported.

The current [GOLD path 5628](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ols4/api/ontologies/gold/terms?iri=https%3A%2F%2Fw3id.org%2Fgold.path%2F5628)
is active, has the exact source path, and explicitly supplies an exact-match
annotation to ENVO:03000040. This supports the seeded mapping rather than
proving it was fabricated. The source's sea-ice context and ENVO's additional
glacier scope deserve ITEM review, but the present evidence does not establish
a separate wrong-identity finding or justify an automatic NARROW remap.

[ENVO:00002200 sea ice](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ols4/api/ontologies/envo/terms?obo_id=ENVO%3A00002200)
is frozen seawater material. A water body located in melting ice is not a kind
of that frozen material. Current ENVO's typed graph distinguishes the target's
water-body subclass edge from adjacency to an ice mass, formation by melting,
and having meltwater as a part. GOLD's contextual parent has become a false
HabitatMech is-a edge.
