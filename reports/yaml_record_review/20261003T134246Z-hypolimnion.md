## Identity and Grounding

Current active ENVO defines hypolimnion as the bottom water layer below the
thermocline in a thermally stratified lake. Its direct parent is
`ENVO:01000283` lake layer, explicitly a layer that is part of a lake.
The vendored edge at `ontology_subclass_edges.tsv:5330` agrees. That relation
does not make the layer a kind of entire freshwater lake (`ENVO:00000021`).
[Hypolimnion](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ols4/api/ontologies/envo/terms?obo_id=ENVO%3A00002130),
[lake layer](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ols4/api/ontologies/envo/terms?obo_id=ENVO%3A01000283),
[freshwater lake](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ols4/api/ontologies/envo/terms?obo_id=ENVO%3A00000021).

`curation/decisions.tsv:347` makes the GOLD slash label exact because
hypolimnion is its head term; row 1432 ITEM-reviews the PREGO self-grounding.
Those two decisions explain `REVIEWED` and the emitted events. They do not
justify the additional lake parent or independently establish equivalence
between thermal hypolimnion and light-defined profundal zone.
