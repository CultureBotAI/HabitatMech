## Identity and Grounding

The exact source path is
`Environmental > Aquatic > Freshwater > Glacier > Ice`. It denotes ice
material in a glacier setting. The generic ice parent `ENVO:01001125` is
strictly broader and compatible with that meaning; the minted identity
preserves the path qualifier. The absence of an ITEM decision agrees with
`SEEDED`, not `REVIEWED`.

Current ENVO distinguishes glacial ice (`ENVO:03000004`), the water-ice
material that is part of a glacier, from glacier (`ENVO:00000133`), a
compound ice mass whose gravity-driven deformation gives it its geographic
identity. Glacial ice is subclassed to water ice (`ENVO:01000277`), then ice.
The source path is a good candidate for glacial-ice grounding, but the
already-true broader ice parent is not itself a wrong mapping merely because
a more specific term is available.
[Glacial ice](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ols4/api/ontologies/envo/terms?obo_id=ENVO%3A03000004),
[water ice](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ols4/api/ontologies/envo/terms?obo_id=ENVO%3A01000277),
[ice](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ols4/api/ontologies/envo/terms?obo_id=ENVO%3A01001125),
[glacier](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ols4/api/ontologies/envo/terms?obo_id=ENVO%3A00000133).

The second parent, `habitatmech:GOLD.6faa98a0aa`, is the source-qualified
whole Glacier record. Material constituting part of that glacier is not
itself a kind of whole glacier. NSIDC independently describes glaciers as
flowing accumulations and distinguishes their constituent ice, supporting
the material/whole distinction rather than a reverse is-a inference.
[NSIDC glacier facts](https://nsidc.org/learn/parts-cryosphere/glaciers/glacier-quick-facts).
