  `Embryo|fish egg-associated environment` as exact synonyms. `Embryo` is the

  kingdom.

  embryo-associated environment`. That misstates both the taxonomic scope and
  the entity type: a bare embryo can be non-fish, and UBERON's `embryo` class is
  the developing organism rather than the microbial habitat determined by a
  fertilized fish egg.
