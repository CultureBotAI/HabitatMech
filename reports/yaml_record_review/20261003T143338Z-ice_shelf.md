organism or anatomical site. `SEEDED` truthfully records the absence of an
ITEM decision. The generated definition is faithful to the vendored ENVO

Current ENVO defines the shelf through coast attachment, thickness, landward
ice outflow and subsequent growth. It directly parents the term to
`ENVO:01001547` land ice mass and `UBERON:0000061` anatomical structure.

broader class of geographic ice shelves. Land ice mass is restricted by its
formation over land. [Ice shelf](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ols4/api/ontologies/envo/terms?obo_id=ENVO%3A00000380),
[land ice mass](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ols4/api/ontologies/envo/terms?obo_id=ENVO%3A01001547),
[anatomical structure](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ols4/api/ontologies/uberon/terms?obo_id=UBERON%3A0000061).

NSIDC recognizes glacier-fed, sea-ice/snowfall-derived and composite shelves.
It explicitly describes Ward Hunt as thickened sea ice and Wilkins as a
composite. These cases challenge universal land-origin/outflow requirements,
not the idea that glacier-fed shelves exist. Its usual thickness range is
not used as a replacement universal lower bound.
[NSIDC ice shelves](https://nsidc.org/learn/parts-cryosphere/ice-shelves).

The third emitted parent, `ENVO:00001999` marine water body, is instead
contributed by GOLD's Marine path. That term denotes a lentic body of marine
water, not an ice shelf floating on it. Location at the coastal ocean does
not supply this is-a relation.
[Marine water body](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ols4/api/ontologies/envo/terms?obo_id=ENVO%3A00001999).
