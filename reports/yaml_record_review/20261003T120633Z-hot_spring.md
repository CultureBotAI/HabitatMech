## Findings

1. **Major: unsupported biome parent.** `parent_habitats` includes
   `ENVO:00002030`, although the grounded entity is a spring landform. GOLD and
   broad-triad ecological context do not establish a strictly broader class.
   Owner: the GOLD source-parent contribution in `src/habitatmech/seed.py`;
   the source-specific exclusion mechanism proposed in draft PR #1218 is a
   suitable maintained input once available. Preserve the independent spring
   parent, exact identity, source path, counts, and reviewed status.
2. **Minor: graph node/predicate typing obscures otherwise plausible biology.**
   `thermophiles` is a physiological grouping, not the organism/clade required
   by `CausalNodeTypeEnum.TAXON`; represent thermophily as a trait or name a
   bounded organism. Likewise, `primary_production_fixes_inorganic_carbon`
   currently says a process `fixes` the process `inorganic_carbon_fixation`,
   rather than fixing carbon or involving carbon fixation. Owner:
   `curation/causal_graphs/hot_spring.yaml`.
3. **Minor: evidence localization and inference scope need tightening.**
   Several supporting snippets are titles instead of claim-bearing passages;
   the temperature-selection citation establishes isolate physiology rather
   than community-wide selection. Power supplies relevant community evidence,
   but its temperature effect is conditional above 70 C. Keep Spear's energy
   conclusion inferential, retain Caldwell's isolate scope, and do not derive
   low-organic environmental measurements from culture growth. Owner: the
   same causal overlay. These qualifications do not invalidate the verified
   autotrophic capabilities or the edges already worded as possibilities.

Blockers: none. Total: one major, two minor.
