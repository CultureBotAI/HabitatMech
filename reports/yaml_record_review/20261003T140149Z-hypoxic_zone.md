## Identity and Grounding

The minted concept denotes a low-oxygen marine zone, not oxygen deficiency
as a quality alone. `curation/decisions.tsv:1377` is explicitly CLASS-depth,
so the generated `SEEDED` status does not claim an individual identity audit.
Retaining a minted ID is defensible while its source scope is unresolved.

The nearby `ENVO:01000065` marine oxygen minimum zone specifically requires
persistent low oxygen and is subclassed to marine water mass and hydroform.
It is not automatically equivalent to every hypoxic zone. NOAA distinguishes
persistent oceanic low-oxygen layers from hypoxia that can vary seasonally.
`ENVO:01001064` hypoxic water is instead an environmental material, not an
exact zone identity. [ENVO oxygen minimum zone](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ols4/api/ontologies/envo/terms?obo_id=ENVO%3A01000065),
[ENVO hypoxic water](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ols4/api/ontologies/envo/terms?obo_id=ENVO%3A01001064),
[NOAA oxygen minimum zones](https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/ocean-fact/omz/),
[NOAA hypoxia](https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/hazards/hypoxia/).

The emitted parent `ENVO:00001999` marine water body is more restrictive
than a generic marine location: ENVO defines it as a lentic water body.
Its lentic genus requires little or no directed flow of the accumulated
water considered in totality. The GOLD label and path do not establish
that condition or establish a water-body identity for the zone. Water
mass is a broader ontology class than water body, not its synonym.
[Marine water body](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ols4/api/ontologies/envo/terms?obo_id=ENVO%3A00001999),
[marine water mass](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ols4/api/ontologies/envo/terms?obo_id=ENVO%3A01000686).
