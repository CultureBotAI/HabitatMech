# PMID:42946263

**Title:** Emergence of high partial nitritation-anammox activity in high-rate nitrifying packed bed biofilm reactor under near-mainstream conditions: Insights into microbial activity and interactions.
**Authors:** Duduku S, Keerthi NS, Ramaswami S
**Journal:** Bioresource technology
**Year:** 2026
**Source:** PubMed https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42946263/, retrieved 2026-11-04

## Abstract

Although partial nitritation and anammox (PN/AMX) has been successfully established for treating sidestream wastewater, low NH4+-N concentration and suppression of nitrite oxidizing bacteria (NOB) are challenges in mainstream wastewater. This study reports the emergence and assessment of PN/AMX-associated activity in single-stage high-rate nitrifying packed bed biofilm reactor (PBBR), operated for ∼ 400 days, with an influent NH4+-N concentration close to mainstream, i.e., 100 mg-N/L, under relatively low hydraulic retention time (∼1.6 h). With gradually increasing volumetric ammonium loading rates (V-ALR) from 300 to 1500 g-N/m3·d during initial stages of operation, dominance of ammonium oxidizing bacteria (AOB) and NOB was achieved, offering complete nitrification. During prolonged operation at V-ALR of 1500 g-N/m3·d, a gradual reduction in dissolved oxygen (DO) saturation in the bulk liquid (at constant aeration rates) to below 45% coincided with NO2--N accumulation, reduced NO3--N formation, and emergence of inferred PN/AMX associated activity. Under DO limited conditions, a peak inferred AMX activity of around 30 mg-N/L·h was observed, with an estimated activity-based doubling time of ∼ 28 d. Microbial community analysis was consistent with these observations, wherein significant reduction in abundance of NOB (Nitrospira) was observed during AMX phase (4.6%) as compared to high-nitrification stage (59.8%). Also, during the AMX phase, the reactor was enriched with Candidatus Brocadia genus with a relative abundance of 20.9%. While these preliminary observations suggest the potential of PBBR for high-rate PN/AMX associated activity, further systematic investigations and validation using real wastewater are needed.
